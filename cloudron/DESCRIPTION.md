SEO Playground is a self-hosted dashboard on top of the [DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com) API. You bring your own DataForSEO account and pay DataForSEO directly for what you use; nothing is sent anywhere else.

* **Rank Tracker** with daily history and the top 10 results of every check
* **Local Finder / Geo-grid** rank maps with scheduled monitors and PDF reports
* **Site Audit**, **Google Reviews** analysis and **web mentions**
* **AI Visibility** and **Prompt Tracker**: see whether ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity mention your brand
* **White-label PDF reports** with your own logo and colours
* Multiple projects, each with its own database, and a spending overview
